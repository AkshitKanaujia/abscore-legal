Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

AbScore ("we", "the app") is a fitness-tracking iOS app. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. We designed AbScore to keep your data on your device.

What we collect and where it lives

On your device only (never sent to our servers):

  • Your logged foods, workouts, water, weight, and body measurements.
  • Your physique scan results and progress photos.
  • Your chat history with the coach.

All of the above is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database. It is not uploaded to us, and we have no account system — we never see it.

Sent for processing, then discarded:

  • Physique scan: when you run a scan, the single photo you choose is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to produce your score. The photo is not stored on our servers or theirs after the response.
  • Coach messages / food photos: the text (and any food photo you attach) is sent to our AI provider to generate the reply, then discarded.
  • Nutrition lookups: food names you ask about may be sent to the USDA FoodData Central database to fetch macros. No personal identifier is attached.

We route these requests through our own secure proxy, which attaches an anonymous per-device identifier used only for rate limiting and abuse prevention. We do not build advertising or tracking profiles, and we do not sell data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple provides us anonymous receipt data to confirm your subscription status.

Third parties

  • Anthropic — AI processing of scans and coach messages (anthropic.com/privacy).
  • USDA FoodData Central — public nutrition database.
  • Apple — app distribution and subscription payments.

Your control

  • Delete everything: Settings → "Delete all my data" removes every scan, photo, meal, workout, and message from your device immediately and permanently.
  • Photos: the app requests camera/photo access only when you choose to add a photo. You can revoke this in iOS Settings at any time.

Age

AbScore is rated 17+ and intended for adults. It is not directed at children.

Not medical advice

AbScore provides general fitness information for motivation. Scores and body-fat figures are visual estimates, not medical measurements. It is not a substitute for professional medical, dietetic, or fitness advice.
